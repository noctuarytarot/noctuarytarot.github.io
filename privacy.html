Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Noctuary Tarot is a simple card-of-the-day site that runs entirely in your browser. This page explains what data the site works with — in short: almost none.

What the site does not do

  • No registration or sign-in
  • No contact form or newsletter
  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own (e.g. Google Analytics)
  • No data stored on a server — the site has no backend

Local storage

To remember your drawn-card history, guide language, and display preferences (such as fullscreen mode), the site stores this information using localStorage directly in your browser. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server. You can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

The site shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads, including personalizing ads based on your previous activity on the internet. These cookies are set and managed by Google, not by the operator of this site — we do not have access to the data Google collects this way.

You can manage ad personalization (for example, turn it off) at Google Ad Settings.

Google Fonts

The site loads fonts (Orbitron, Cormorant Garamond) from Google Fonts. When the page loads, your browser connects to Google's servers, which may involve sending your IP address. This is not cross-site tracking or used for marketing — it is purely the technical delivery of fonts. You can find more about how Google handles this data in Google's own privacy policy.

Cookies

The site itself does not set any cookies of its own. Cookies related to ads are set by Google, as described above.

Links to other sites

The site includes links to the SYNTHLUCIDA Relax Player. This policy covers Noctuary Tarot only — external sites have their own privacy policies.

A note on the readings

Noctuary Tarot is offered as a daily space for pause, reflection, and atmosphere — it is not fortune-telling, a prediction of the future, or a substitute for professional guidance on important life decisions.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, you can reach us through the links on the Noctuary Tarot homepage.
