FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions & answers

What is Noctuary Tarot? ▾

Noctuary Tarot is a web app for a card of the day. Draw one card from the classic 78-card Tarot, the Cosmic Whisper Oracle, or the mystical Night Tarot and read its meaning in English and Czech.

Is it free? ▾

Yes, Noctuary Tarot is completely free and works right in your browser, with no registration and nothing to download.

What's the difference between the decks? ▾

Tarot is the classic 78-card deck with traditional meanings, Oracle is looser and more intuitive, and Night Tarot is an original mystical deck with a darker atmosphere.

What does a reversed card mean? ▾

A reversed card usually points to a blocked, weakened, or inward version of its upright meaning — for example hesitation instead of certainty, or a theme that's only just asking for attention.

Does the app save my card history? ▾

Yes, your drawn card history stays saved in the browser and is always available through the clock icon.

Does Noctuary Tarot also have a horoscope? ▾

Yes — tap the ✦ icon or visit the horoscope page for a free monthly horoscope covering all 12 zodiac signs.
