HOW TO PLAY

Night Rain is played with a 40-card deck — four suits (Moon, Star, Flame, Drop), each numbered 1 to 10. Be the first to play every card from your hand.

MATCHING

On your turn, play a card that shares either the suit or the number with the top card of the discard pile. If you have nothing to play, draw one card — if it can be played, you may play it right away.

7 — DOWNPOUR

Playing a 7 sends the Downpour to your opponent: they must draw two cards and lose their turn, unless they play a 7 of their own to send it back and add two more.

1 — ECLIPSE

Playing a 1 covers the table in an Eclipse: your opponent's next turn is skipped and play returns straight to you.

10 — FULL MOON

A 10 is a Full Moon — it can be played on anything, and lets you choose which suit comes next.

WINNING

The first player to run out of cards wins the round. Tap NEW GAME any time to shuffle and start over.

About Night Rain

Night Rain (Noční liják) is an original card game from the makers of Noctuary Tarot, built for anyone who enjoys quick, tactical shedding games — the kind of family card game played across many countries under many different names, including the Czech classic Prší. Night Rain keeps that same easy-to-learn, hard-to-put-down spirit, with its own four-suit deck and its own set of special cards.

The whole match runs in your browser. There is nothing to install, no account to create, and no time limit — deal a new hand whenever you like.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Night Rain?

A free browser card game inspired by classic shedding card games such as Prší. Match the suit or number of the top card and be the first to empty your hand.

Is it free to play?

Yes, completely free, with no download, account or sign-up required.

How many players are supported?

This version is single-player — you play one on one against a computer opponent.

What do the special cards do?

A 7 is a Downpour that makes the next player draw two, a 1 is an Eclipse that skips a turn, and a 10 is a Full Moon wild card that lets you choose the next suit.

Is Night Rain the same game as Prší?

Night Rain is an original reskin inspired by the same family of games as Prší, with its own four suits, a 1-10 deck and renamed special cards, rather than a direct copy.

Night Rain is offered as a free, light-hearted diversion — there is no real-money play involved and no data is required to play.
